About this Speed Tester

Many speed testers in South Asia or emerging markets route requests to BDIX (Bangladesh Internet Exchange) or local ISP caching servers. While this shows extremely high speeds (often matching your router link speed), it does not reflect your actual speed when browsing global websites like YouTube, Netflix, GitHub, or international cloud servers.

This tool uses direct international CDN files hosted on Cloudflare, Fastly/jsDelivr, and Google to perform the test. Because CDNs are the backbone of the global internet, downloading from them gives you a 100% accurate measurement of your real-world international browsing and streaming speed.
